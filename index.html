Entry 01, on perception

Some people build portfolios. I built an archive. Because the work I like doesn't begin with content. It begins with perception. With what people assume, repeat, and remember long after the campaign is over.

I'm drawn to brands that are tired of being safe. Brands that need a sharper voice, a stronger presence, and a narrative that doesn't dissolve the moment the scroll moves on.

This diary holds the proof. Pitch decks, writing, films, ads, and work that has already lived in public. Some pages are polished. Some are messy. But nothing here exists without intent.

Script Craft

Three scripts, three voices.

Script 1

Puraromas

Creator testimonial for a cold and flu relief spray

“Don't wait another season to breathe freely. Try Puraroma today and feel the difference.”

Read the full script

Quick montage: tissues piling up, alarm clock showing 3 AM, creator tossing and turning.

Hook (VO)Struggling to breathe at night? Allergies making your days miserable?

Scene 1: Creator sneezing, red nose, tissues everywhere.

VOThis used to be me every single allergy season. Stuffy nose, constant sneezing, barely able to sleep.

Scene 2: Cut to somebody passing tissues or checking in. (Concerned)

VOIt got so bad, everybody started worrying about me. They kept telling me I needed to find something that actually works.

Scene 3: Creator scrolling on phone, discovering Puraroma.

VOOne day, while searching online, I came across Puraroma Cold & Flu Relief Spray.

Scene 4: Holds up spray.

VOIt's made with eucalyptus and peppermint oil, both known to open up airways and ease congestion. And honestly? For the first time in years, I felt like I could actually breathe like a normal person again.

Scene 5: Creator outside enjoying fresh air with everybody smiling in the background.

Creator (talking to camera)It's natural, easy to use, and a total lifesaver.

CTA (Scene 6): Product close-up with text on screen.

CTADon't wait another season to breathe freely. Try Puraroma today and feel the difference.

Script 2

Swiggy

Comedy sketch: two students, one empty fridge

“Swiggy, delivering the end to bachelor hunger.”

Read the full script

Characters: 2 students, Y and Z.

Scene: A dimly lit, slightly messy bachelor apartment. Books, laptop, gaming gear, headphones scattered around. Student Y slouching on the couch.

Y (groaning)Bhai, bhuk lagi haii!

Z (looking up from his gaming console)Fridge check kar.

Scene: Y gets up lazily, walks to the fridge and opens it. Close-up of a ketchup bottle, half a lemon, and a few sad fenugreek leaves that look like they have been there for months.

Y (deadpans)Nothing… but disappointment!

Z (smirks)Guess we are cooking.

Y (sarcastically)Ha.

Scene: Z grabs his phone and opens the Swiggy app. Cut to a quick shot of the doorbell ringing and a delivery bag being handed over to Y!

CTASwiggy, delivering the end to bachelor hunger.
